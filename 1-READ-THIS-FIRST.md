## What it actually does

Here's the problem nobody talks about:

Your AI is smart, but it has no idea where anything lives.

So every new chat, you re-explain your business. You re-paste your brand voice. You hunt for that one file. You lose the thread from last session.

This kit gives your AI the map:

- A folder for what you're working on right now
- A folder where every work session leaves a note for the next one
- A folder for each of your brands — voice, customer, rules
- A ready-made set of folders for your Google Drive (different shape, same labels — explained below)

## Two structures, two jobs (read this so it doesn't confuse you)

You'll notice the workspace folders and the Google Drive folders are NOT identical. That's on purpose.

The workspace is your AI's brain. It holds words — notes, plans, session history, brand voice. Your AI reads and writes here.

Google Drive is your filing cabinet. It holds things — images, PDFs, contracts, photos, media.

A brain and a filing cabinet shouldn't be organized the same way. What DOES stay identical across both:

- Your brand names — `Acme-Marketing` in the workspace is `Acme-Marketing` in Drive, letter for letter
- The habits — numbered folders, inbox at the top, archive at the bottom, dates as YYYY-MM-DD

So when your AI says "the logo is in Brand-One/branding," that's true in Drive. And when you say "check my brand voice," your AI knows that lives in the workspace. Different rooms, same labels.

## The one rule I'll give you up front

Name your files starting with the date, like this: `2026-06-12-invoice.pdf`

Year, then month, then day. It's the only date format that sorts itself — in Drive, on your computer, everywhere. Do this one thing and you'll never scroll around looking for "the latest version" again.
