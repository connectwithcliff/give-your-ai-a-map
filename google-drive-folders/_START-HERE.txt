HOW TO USE THIS

1. Open drive.google.com in your browser.
2. Drag the numbered folders (00-inbox through backups) from inside this
   folder straight into Drive. That's it - the structure uploads itself.
3. Rename Brand-One and Brand-Two (inside 02-business) to your real brands.
   Use the SAME names in your AI workspace and your email labels.
4. Each folder has an _ABOUT.txt explaining what goes in it. Once you know
   the system, delete them - or keep them as reminders.

The two rules that make it work:
- Numbers force order: inbox on top, archive on the bottom.
- Dates are YYYY-MM-DD, always: 2026-06-12-invoice.pdf sorts itself forever.
